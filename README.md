# RISE OF THE DON

*THE CITY NEVER FORGIVES.*

An original 3D open-world action-adventure set in **Velanagar**, a fictional South Indian coastal city. Built with Godot 4 (GDScript only), with placeholder 3D art generated by Blender scripts.

**Status: Milestone 7 (expansion) complete, awaiting review.** You are **Vetri**, off the bus in Old Town with ₹200.
- **The city:** Velanagar has six districts: Old Town in the middle; **Downtown** to the north, with glass towers, a mall, a plaza and neon at night; the **Beach District** to the east, with the sea, a pier and a lighthouse; the **Industrial Zone** to the north-east, with factories and the harbour; the **Luxury District** to the north-west, with walled villas and a mansion estate; and the **Countryside** to the west and south-west, with paddy fields, villages and lakes. Their streets run into each other: the whole city is about 1.5 km across.
- **Story:** fifteen story missions, from your first parcel to the fight for the harbour, with cutscenes, fights, tails and escapes, and two endings; plus two repeatable side jobs.
- **Police:** crimes bring a wanted level (one to five stars) and police who chase, block roads and arrest you.
- **Fists:** punch people (and get punched); knocked out, you wake in hospital, minus a bill.
- **Shops:** a tiffin stall restores health, a clothes shop sells outfits, and a garage resprays your car and clears up to three stars.
- **The house:** once bought, it's a save point and pays rent.
- **Day and night, and weather:** a 24-minute cycle that lights up the town after dark; spells of clear, cloudy, foggy and rainy weather blend into each other, and rain wets the streets.
- **Navigation:** a minimap and a full map with waypoints and GPS.
- **Saving:** three slots plus an autosave.

**Old Town** is a 512 m × 512 m temple town streamed in 128 m chunks around you:
- a walled temple compound with four gopurams, ringed by narrow lanes,
- main roads with traffic lights,
- a market square, a temple tank and parks,
- about 1,150 buildings.

The streets around you are alive:
- **Traffic** keeps left, stops at red lights and give-way lines, and keeps its distance. It changes lanes to pass parked cars and honks when held up.
- **Pedestrians** walk the sidewalks, cross at zebras, chat in pairs and sit on benches. They run from crashes and step aside for horns, and a car can knock them down.

You can walk, run, sprint and jump, or get into a parked vehicle and drive (chase and hood cameras, speedometer, horn, headlights, crash damage). Besides the sedans there are five more: a sports car outside the estate (Luxury District), an SUV by Central Plaza (Downtown), the city bus at the Old Town bus stand, a truck on the harbour road (Industrial Zone), and a motorcycle outside the tiffin stall opposite the bus stand. Sports cars, SUVs and motorcycles also turn up in traffic. See [docs/GAME_DESIGN.md](docs/GAME_DESIGN.md) for the full design and milestone plan, and [docs/PERFORMANCE.md](docs/PERFORMANCE.md) for performance figures.

## Open and run

1. Start the Godot editor and choose **Import**, then select this folder's `project.godot`.
2. Press **F5** (or the ▶ Play button). The game starts at the main menu.

From the command line (Git Bash), without opening the editor:

```bash
GODOT="/c/Users/ADMIN/Downloads/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe"
"$GODOT" --path .
```

The window opens at 1600×900; the game renders at a 1920×1080 base resolution and scales to the window.

## Build a Windows version

With the Godot 4.7.2 export templates installed:

```bash
GODOT="/c/Users/ADMIN/Downloads/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe"
"$GODOT" --headless --path . --export-release "Windows Desktop" build/windows/RiseOfTheDon.exe
```

This writes `build/windows/RiseOfTheDon.exe` and `RiseOfTheDon.pck`. Keep the two files together; copy that folder to another PC and run the `.exe`. The preset is in `export_presets.cfg` (tests, tools and docs are left out of the build).

## The city

Velanagar is laid out on a grid of 512 m squares, one district each, with Old Town in the middle (north is up on the map). Crossing into another district shows its name at the top of the screen, and the full map (M) shows every district with its name.

**Old Town**
- **The start:** you step off the bus at the Old Town bus stand, on the sidewalk of the road leading to the temple's east gopuram. A sedan is parked against the kerb further along. Two more sedans are parked around town: on the southern ring road (Ring B, west side) and on the road north of the temple.
- **Getting around:** traffic keeps left. The main roads (four lanes) run through the gopuram axes and around two rings, with traffic lights where two main roads cross. Everything between is narrow two-lane lanes.
- **Landmarks:** the market square is north-west of the temple, and the stepped temple tank is south-east.

**Downtown** (north of Old Town)
- Every north-south street of Old Town carries on into Downtown. The road north from the temple's north gopuram is the quickest way in.
- Glass office towers up to 120 m tall, mid-rise offices, shops, a hotel, Vela Mall and Central Plaza with its fountain. The police headquarters is on the central east-west road, just west of Central Plaza; an arrest ends at whichever police station is nearer.
- At night, office floors light up one by one and the shop signs glow. From Old Town, the Downtown towers stand on the skyline.

**Beach District** (east of Old Town)
- Old Town's east-west streets carry on into it; the main roads go on to the Coast Road along the seafront.
- The Coast Road is lined with seafood restaurants, small hotels and beach shops; two Sea Breeze resorts face the sea across it. Cottages and pastel houses fill the lanes, and there's a police post.
- Across the road: the promenade (palms, benches, kiosks), the sand (umbrellas, fishing boats, lifeguard towers), a wooden pier you can walk out on, and the red-and-white lighthouse at the south end. Its beam turns at night.
- You can wade about 40 m into the sea; further out is too deep. Pedestrians stay on the sand.

**Industrial Zone** (north of the Beach, east of Downtown)
- Downtown's east-west streets and the Beach's roads carry on into it; the Coast Road continues north as the harbour road.
- Warehouses, a brick factory with a tall chimney, tank farms and site offices on big yards. A police post.
- The harbour: a container yard on the quay, three gantry cranes over a moored cargo ship, and a fishing harbour with a fish-market shed and boats at the south end.
- At night the streets glow sodium orange.

**Luxury District** (west of Downtown)
- Downtown's east-west streets carry on into it. Quiet avenues lined with walled villa plots (gates, lawns, pools); guard booths where the two main roads come in from the south.
- The estate in the middle: a walled mansion with a colonnade, a fountain court, a gatehouse and garages. Sandalwood Park beside it.
- No police station: the district has its own guards. An arrest here ends at the nearest station.

**Countryside** (west and south-west of Old Town; twice the size of the other districts)
- A country lane along Old Town's west edge collects its streets; the highway (Old Town's middle road) and the two ring roads go on west. The Luxury District's two main roads run south through the whole Countryside.
- Paddy fields with farmsteads and haystacks, coconut groves, a lake and a reservoir with benches, and two villages (round huts, farmhouses, a tea stall, a shrine and a well). The northern village has a police post.
- No streetlights: nights out here are dark.

**The edge:** walls stop you at the edge of the city. South of Old Town and the Beach there is no district yet, so roads there end at the city's edge.
- **Wrecking the car:** if a car ends up on its side or roof while you're in it, it rights itself after a couple of seconds. Crashes cost the car health (the CAR bar under the speedometer). Below about a third it smokes, and at zero the engine dies until you start a new game.

## Missions, cash and saving

**How missions work**
- **The HUD:** the current mission and objective sit under the health bars (with a clock when there is a time limit). A cyan beam marks where to go, a cyan diamond floats over the person to talk to or the car to get in, and an arrow at the edge of the screen points the way when it's off screen.
- **Starting one:** a magenta beam marks a mission you can start. Walk into it on foot (the person offering it stands beside it).
- **Talking:** walk up to someone and press **E**. **E** also skips a line of dialogue.
- **Failing:** if a mission fails (for example, the borrowed car is wrecked or time runs out), a screen offers **Retry** from the last checkpoint or **Quit Mission**. A quit mission goes back on offer.
- **Cutscenes:** some story moments play as cutscenes: letterbox bars, moving camera shots, subtitles in the bottom bar, and people who walk and talk. **E** skips to the next shot.
- **Fights:** some missions have you knock out a group of thugs; they show as red triangles on the map, and the objective counts how many are left.
- **Tailing:** follow a car without being seen. Stay within sight (the objective warns "Don't lose them!") but not on its bumper ("Too close!"): too close for a few seconds and they spot you.
- **Escapes:** get out of the red circle on the map, sometimes with people chasing you on foot.
- **Choices:** at a few moments the story asks you to decide. The game waits for your answer, and it changes what happens next (it is saved with your game).

**The missions so far** (the characters are original: Vetri, the fixer Durai, Rafiq the tailor, Meena, Kamalam who runs the flower market, Selvi at the tiffin stall and Murugan the mechanic)
1. **Welcome to Velanagar** (starts on New Game, at the Old Town bus stand). Walk to the temple's east gate, into the courtyard, then to the temple tank. Reward ₹300.

3. **The New Connection** (Durai at the tank). Meet Kamalam at the market hall, north-west of the temple. The meeting plays as a scene: a fixed camera, letterbox bars, and you stand and listen (**E** moves on a line). Reward ₹1,500. Afterwards Velanagar Textiles serves you, and Selvi's and Murugan's side jobs open.
4. **City Chase** (Kamalam at the market hall). Get into the watched black car and you get two stars at once.
   - Drive through five checkpoints round Ring B, in order, within 150 s; you don't need to stop.
   - Then lose the police: break their line of sight and stay out of the red circle, or use the garage respray.
   - Finally, leave the car in Murugan's garage. Wreck it or get busted and the mission fails.
   - Reward ₹2,000. Afterwards the verandah house goes on sale.
5. **Building an Empire** (Durai at the verandah house north of Ring B). It only starts once you have ₹6,000; side jobs make up the difference. Talk to Durai, then buy the house at its door. Reward ₹1,000.

7. **The Night Van** (Kamalam). Take Murugan's motorbike and tail the white van that collects for Periyasamy, from the market to Downtown, without being seen. A cutscene shows where the money goes. ₹3,000.

11. **The Detective** (Nandini at the Old Town police station). Tail Bala's black SUV in Nandini's car to the Beach. Watch Periyasamy at the lighthouse, then get away from his men. ₹3,000.

After the last mission the city stays open to explore, and the side jobs carry on.

**Cash** is shown top right and counts up when you're paid.

**Saving**
- **Save:** pause → **Save Game**, then pick slot 1, 2 or 3 (an occupied slot asks you to pick it again before overwriting).
- **Autosave:** the game also autosaves after every mission passed.
- **Load:** on the title screen, **Continue** loads the newest save and **Load Game** lists all four.
- **What's kept:** where you were, health, cash, missions passed, play time and stats.
- **Saving during a mission:** loading puts you back at the mission's last checkpoint.
- **Where the files are:** `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Rise of the Don\saves\slot_0.json` (the autosave) to `slot_3.json`.

Missions are data. Edit `resources/missions/m0*.tres` and `job_*.tres` in the inspector.
- **What's editable:** objectives (go to, talk to, get in, checkpoints, lose the police, buy a property), positions, dialogue (and whether it plays as a scene), time limits, checkpoints, wanted levels and rewards.
- **Missions themselves:** the cash a mission needs before it starts, and whether it repeats.
- **The story list:** `resources/missions/story.tres` lists the missions and sets the starting cash.

## Life in the city

Cars and people appear out of sight in a ring around you and are removed once you've left them far behind. So the town always feels busy where you are, while only the nearby streets are simulated. Press **F3** to see how many are about.

**Traffic.** Every traffic car is a full physics sedan with an AI driver, so you can bump, ram and wreck it like your own car. Drivers:
- follow the lanes and keep left;
- choose straight on, left or right at junctions;
- stop on red (and on amber when they can), and give way where a lane meets a main road;
- wait before turning right across oncoming traffic;
- keep a gap to whatever is in front, including you and pedestrians;
- on the four-lane main roads, change lanes to get past a parked or wrecked car;
- honk after a few seconds stuck behind something, and stop for a moment after a crash.

**Pedestrians.** People:
- wander the sidewalks, crossing roads only at crossings;
- stop for a while, chat face to face in pairs, and sit on benches (temple courtyard, parks, market, tank yard);
- run from crashes and from someone being knocked down;
- step aside when a horn sounds in front of them or when you walk into them.

A car hitting someone at speed knocks them down. They lie on the road for a few seconds, then get up and run.

## Shops, the house and side jobs

You play **Vetri**, new in the city. Walk up to a shop door (green ring) and press **E** to open its counter. The game pauses while you choose, and **Leave** or Esc closes it. On the map, shops show as green badges.

| Place | Map | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bus Stand Tiffin Stall ("TIFFIN") | F | Across the road from the bus stand | Food restores health: coffee +10 (₹20), idli and vada +35 (₹50), full meals to full (₹120) |
| Velanagar Textiles | C | Ring B, west of the police station (serves you after The New Connection) | Six outfits (₹600–₹3,500). Buy once, then change into any you own for free |
| East Road Garage ("GARAGE") | G | Far east on the east gopuram road | Drive into the bay, stop, press **E**: new paint, repairs and new tyres for ₹500, and up to 3 stars lost. Not with 4+ stars, or while the police can see you |
| Verandah house | ₹ / H | North of Ring B, east side (FOR SALE board after City Chase) | ₹6,000. Once yours, **E** at the door saves the game, and it pays ₹400 rent every in-game day |

**Side jobs** (on offer after The New Connection; repeat them as often as you like):
- **Tiffin Delivery** (Selvi, at the tiffin stall): take a hot lunch to a customer within 100 s. You can run or use any car. Pays ₹250.
- **Garage Run** (Murugan, at the garage): drive a customer's car to its owner within 120 s without wrecking it. Pays ₹600.
- Each run picks a different customer.

What you own and what you're wearing are saved. Shop stock, prices, outfits and the house are data: `resources/shops/*.tres`, `resources/properties/verandah_house.tres`, `resources/missions/job_*.tres`.

## Day, night, the map and the police

**Day and night.**
- A full day lasts 24 real minutes. The clock is shown under the cash and is saved with the game.
- After dark, lamps and lit windows come on across town and traffic drives with headlights.
- The lighting presets are in `resources/world/day_night.tres`.

**Sound.** Each district has its own background: traffic in Old Town and Downtown (quieter at night), machinery and clanks in the Industrial Zone, waves along the coast, birdsong in the Luxury District and the Countryside (crickets at night). Inside a vehicle it's muffled. Footsteps sound like the ground: road, paving, sand, grass, the pier's boards, a splash in the sea. The mix is in `resources/world/ambience.tres`; every sound is synthesised in code (`scripts/systems/audio_synth.gd`).

**Weather.**
- Spells of clear, cloudy, foggy and rainy weather last a few minutes each and blend into one another over about 45 seconds.
- Cloud covers the sky and dims the sun; fog thickens the haze; rain falls around you, is heard, and leaves the streets dark and shiny. They dry off slowly once it stops.
- The weather is saved with the game. The kinds of weather, how likely each is and how long spells last are in `resources/world/weather.tres`.

**Map and GPS.**
- **Minimap** (bottom left): turns with you, zooms out as you drive faster, and shows the GPS route, mission blips, police and the search area.
- **Full map:** **M** (or pause → Map) opens the whole city with district and place names.
- **Waypoints:** click a spot on the full map to set a waypoint. The GPS route follows the roads in the legal direction of travel.

**Wanted level** (five stars under the clock; tuning in `resources/police/wanted.tres`):
- **Crimes:** knocking someone down with a car, ramming a police car, running over an officer, punching people in the street (two punches make one star) or punching an officer makes you wanted, and more crimes raise the level.
- **Escalation:** at levels 1–4, staying in sight of the police long enough also raises the level.

| Level | Police response |
|---|---|
| 1 | Officers on foot |
| 2 | Patrol cars (sirens and flashing light bars); a car pulling up beside you on foot lets two officers out |
| 3 | Roadblocks across the road ahead when you drive fast |
| 4 | Spike strips (yellow and black) before the roadblocks; driving over one bursts your tyres |
| 5 | The helicopter, circling overhead with a searchlight; it sees a long way |

**Losing the police.**
- When no unit can see you, the stars blink and a red circle on the minimap shows where they're searching. The circle shrinks over time.
- Stay outside it for 8 seconds to drop a star. Repeat until the stars are gone.

**Busted.**
- An officer catching you on foot, or police surrounding your stopped car, arrests you.
- You pay a fine (10% of your cash, at least ₹100) and are released outside the police station on Ring B (blue "POLICE" sign, marked on the map).
- A mission you were on fails.

## Fights and the hospital

**Punching.** Left mouse (gamepad B) throws a punch. You turn to the nearest person just in front of you, and your fists stay up for a moment afterwards. You can't punch while driving. Tuning is in `resources/characters/player_tuning.tres`.
- **People in the street** reel back from a punch and run. A quick second punch floors them for a few seconds, and everyone nearby runs. Each punch is an assault: two make one star.
- **Police officers** take three punches to floor. The first punch is assaulting an officer (two stars at once).
- **Fighters** are thugs who fight back; the story missions bring them. They close in with their fists up, draw back an arm (your cue to hit first or step away) and swing. Hitting one mid-wind-up breaks it. Four punches knock one out for good. Tuning is in `resources/characters/fighter.tres`.

**Getting hurt.** A punch costs health, pushes you back and flashes the screen edges red. Health creeps back up to half on its own; the tiffin stall's food restores the rest.

**Hospitalised.** At no health you go down. After a moment the **HOSPITALISED** card shows and you wake at the nearest hospital at full health:
- the **clinic** on Old Town's west gopuram road, or **Velanagar General Hospital** east of Central Plaza, Downtown;
- both are marked on the map, with a green cross on the building;
- the bill is 10% of your cash, at least ₹100;
- the police lose interest (your stars are cleared);
- a mission you were on fails.

## Settings

Changes apply immediately and are saved to `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Rise of the Don\settings.cfg` when you leave the screen.

|---|---:|---:|
| Low | 7 | 12 |

**Graphics** (in the same screen; scroll down):
- **Graphics quality:** a preset that sets the options below.
- **Ambient occlusion:** soft shadows in corners and under things.
- **Glow:** bloom around lamps, windows and neon.
- **Shadows and lights:** how sharp the sun's shadows are, how far they reach, and how many streetlights cast real light at night.
- **Render scale:** below 100% the 3D view renders smaller and is upscaled with AMD FSR.
- **VSync** and **Fullscreen**.

## Regenerating assets

All 3D models come from Python scripts in `tools/blender/`. To rebuild every model and re-import:

```bash
bash tools/blender/build_all.sh
```

Each generator can also be run alone and accepts asset names, e.g.
`"$BLENDER" --background --factory-startup --python tools/blender/buildings.py -- bld_shophouse_02`.
The scripts are deterministic: re-running produces identical files.

The city itself is generated from those models by a Godot script (`build_all.sh` runs it last):

```bash
"$GODOT_CONSOLE" --headless --path . --script res://tools/godot/build_city.gd
```

It does six things:
1. Bakes each model it uses into a single-surface mesh in `assets/world/kit/`.
2. Lays out the city with `scripts/world/city_layout.gd`: each district has its own deterministic layout script (`old_town_layout.gd`, `downtown_layout.gd`, sharing `district_layout.gd`), and roads that reach a shared edge are joined.
3. Writes a chunk scene for every 128 m chunk inside a district to `scenes/world/chunks/city/`, with collision and a baked pedestrian navmesh.
4. Writes the always-loaded landmark scene: the temple, simple stand-ins for the tall towers (so the skyline shows from afar), and the walls and ground at the city's edge.
5. Writes the lane graph and the city map image (`assets/world/maps/city_map.png`; Godot compresses it for the GPU on import).
6. Writes the city manifest, `resources/world/city.tres` (districts, police stations, shop and property doors).

Don't edit the generated files by hand: change a layout script or the Blender models and rebuild (about 5 seconds).

The input map, autoloads and audio buses are generated by `tools/godot/setup_project.gd`. Edit that script rather than the `[input]` section of `project.godot`, then run:

```bash
"$GODOT_CONSOLE" --headless --path . --script res://tools/godot/setup_project.gd
```

(`$GODOT_CONSOLE` is the `_console.exe` next to the editor executable.)
