# RISE OF THE DON

*THE CITY NEVER FORGIVES.*

An original 3D open-world action-adventure set in **Velanagar**, a fictional South Indian coastal city. Built with Godot 4 (GDScript only), with placeholder 3D art generated by Blender scripts.

**Status: Milestone 4 (life in the city).** Main menu → New Game drops you into **Old Town**, a 512 m × 512 m temple town streamed in 128 m chunks around you:
- a walled temple compound with four gopurams, ringed by narrow lanes,
- main roads with traffic lights,
- a market square, a temple tank and parks,
- about 1,150 buildings.

The streets around you are alive:
- **Traffic** keeps left, stops at red lights and give-way lines, and keeps its distance. It changes lanes to pass parked cars and honks when held up.
- **Pedestrians** walk the sidewalks, cross at zebras, chat in pairs and sit on benches. They run from crashes and step aside for horns, and a car can knock them down.

You can walk, run, sprint and jump, or get into one of the parked sedans and drive (chase and hood cameras, speedometer, horn, headlights, crash damage). See [docs/GAME_DESIGN.md](docs/GAME_DESIGN.md) for the full design and milestone plan, and [docs/PERFORMANCE.md](docs/PERFORMANCE.md) for performance figures.

## Open and run

1. Start the Godot editor and choose **Import**, then select this folder's `project.godot`.
2. Press **F5** (or the ▶ Play button). The game starts at the main menu.

From the command line (Git Bash), without opening the editor:

```bash
GODOT="/c/Users/ADMIN/Downloads/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64.exe"
"$GODOT" --path .
```

The window opens at 1600×900; the game renders at a 1920×1080 base resolution and scales to the window.

## Old Town

- **The start:** you begin on the road leading to the temple's east gopuram, with a sedan parked against the left kerb a few metres ahead. Two more sedans are parked around town: on the southern ring road (Ring B, west side) and on the road north of the temple.
- **Getting around:** traffic keeps left. The main roads (four lanes) run through the gopuram axes and around two rings, with traffic lights where two main roads cross. Everything between is narrow two-lane lanes.
- **Landmarks:** the market square is north-west of the temple, and the stepped temple tank is south-east.
- **The edge:** invisible walls stop you at the edge of the district.
- **Wrecking the car:** if a car ends up on its side or roof while you're in it, it rights itself after a couple of seconds. Crashes cost the car health (the CAR bar under the speedometer). Below about a third it smokes, and at zero the engine dies until you start a new game.

## Life in the city

Cars and people appear out of sight in a ring around you and are removed once you've left them far behind. So the town always feels busy where you are, while only the nearby streets are simulated. Press **F3** to see how many are about.

**Traffic.** Every traffic car is a full physics sedan with an AI driver, so you can bump, ram and wreck it like your own car. Drivers:
- follow the lanes and keep left;
- choose straight on, left or right at junctions;
- stop on red (and on amber when they can), and give way where a lane meets a main road;
- wait before turning right across oncoming traffic;
- keep a gap to whatever is in front, including you and pedestrians;
- on the four-lane main roads, change lanes to get past a parked or wrecked car;
- honk after a few seconds stuck behind something, and stop for a moment after a crash.

**Pedestrians.** People:
- wander the sidewalks, crossing roads only at crossings;
- stop for a while, chat face to face in pairs, and sit on benches (temple courtyard, parks, market, tank yard);
- run from crashes and from someone being knocked down;
- step aside when a horn sounds in front of them or when you walk into them.

A car hitting someone at speed knocks them down. They lie on the road for a few seconds, then get up and run.

Changes apply immediately and are saved to `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Rise of the Don\settings.cfg` when you leave the screen.

## Project layout

```

             world/ (old_town.tscn, the M1 test block), world/chunks/old_town/ (generated chunk scenes)
scripts/     player/, vehicles/, ui/, systems/ (Settings and AudioManager autoloads, game session, camera hand-over),
             world/ (streamer, district, layout, traffic signals, lane graph, kit baker),
             npc/ (traffic and pedestrian managers, traffic driver, pedestrian, character baker, tunings)
resources/   tunables: characters/, vehicles/, world/ (lighting presets, signal timing, Old Town manifest and lane graph)
assets/      models/ (generated GLBs: road/, buildings/, props/, vehicles/, characters/, test/), world/kit/ (baked district meshes)
shaders/     menu background, palette material for district meshes and car paint, animated pedestrians
tests/       smoke test, player, vehicle, traffic and pedestrian tests, performance baseline, screenshot tool
tools/       blender/ (asset generators), godot/ (project setup script, Old Town builder)
docs/        GAME_DESIGN.md, PERFORMANCE.md
```

Tuning lives in resources, not code. Edit these in the Godot inspector:
- `resources/characters/player_tuning.tres`: speeds, jump, stamina, getting in and out of vehicles
- `resources/characters/player_camera.tres`: on-foot camera distance, sensitivity, smoothing
- `resources/vehicles/sedan_stats.tres`: the sedan's mass, engine, brakes, steering, suspension, tyre grip, gearbox sound, damage
- `resources/vehicles/vehicle_camera.tres`: chase distance, swing-back delay, speed field of view, hood view
- `resources/world/old_town_afternoon.tres`: Old Town's sun, sky and haze (`late_afternoon.tres` is the M1 test block's)
- `resources/world/signal_timing.tres`: traffic-light green, amber and all-red times
- `resources/characters/population.tres`: how many cars and people at each density, where they appear and disappear, car paint and clothing colours
- `resources/vehicles/traffic_driver.tres`: how AI drivers drive (cruise speed, braking, following gap, junctions, passing, patience)
- `resources/characters/pedestrian.tres`: walking and running speeds, wandering, idling, sitting and chatting times, reactions
- Chunk streaming distances: the `WorldStreamer` node in `scenes/world/old_town.tscn` (load 200 m, unload 250 m)

## Regenerating assets

All 3D models come from Python scripts in `tools/blender/`. To rebuild every model and re-import:

```bash
bash tools/blender/build_all.sh
```

Each generator can also be run alone and accepts asset names, e.g.
`"$BLENDER" --background --factory-startup --python tools/blender/buildings.py -- bld_shophouse_02`.
The scripts are deterministic: re-running produces identical files.

Old Town itself is generated from those models by a Godot script (`build_all.sh` runs it last):

```bash
"$GODOT_CONSOLE" --headless --path . --script res://tools/godot/build_old_town.gd
```

It does five things:
1. Bakes each model it uses into a single-surface mesh in `assets/world/kit/`.
2. Lays out the town with `scripts/world/old_town_layout.gd`, which is deterministic.
3. Writes the 16 chunk scenes and the temple landmark scene to `scenes/world/chunks/old_town/`, with collision and a baked pedestrian navmesh.
4. Writes the lane graph.
5. Writes the district manifest, `resources/world/old_town.tres`.

Don't edit the generated files by hand: change the layout script or the Blender models and rebuild (about 5 seconds).

The input map, autoloads and audio buses are generated by `tools/godot/setup_project.gd`. Edit that script rather than the `[input]` section of `project.godot`, then run:

```bash
"$GODOT_CONSOLE" --headless --path . --script res://tools/godot/setup_project.gd
```

(`$GODOT_CONSOLE` is the `_console.exe` next to the editor executable.)
